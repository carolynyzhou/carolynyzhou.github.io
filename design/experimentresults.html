Test Results Dashboard

Problem

Data analysts created ad hoc results dashboards after every test, each with its own layout and metric definitions. For leadership and partner reviews, this meant re-learning the dashboard every time and creating high cognitive overhead.

Outcome

A consistent, intuitive A/B readout built for leadership and partner reviews, surfacing statistical confidence and a clear recommendation up front.

My RoleLead Designer

AudienceLeadership & partner reviews

Background

Before: Ad Hoc by Default

Every team that ran a test built its own dashboard, and every dashboard made its own call on what counted as a win. Some charted lift against a baseline window, others against a rolling average, and a handful of metrics didn't match their own definitions from one review to the next.

Leadership sat through reviews where the first few minutes were spent just orienting to a new layout before anyone could get to the actual decision. The redesign needed a single template that leadership and partner teams could read the same way, review after review.

  • Outcome/launch decision (or status/progress)
  • Test results
  • Hypothesis
  • Treatments + control
  • Experiment + creator name
  • Request info
  • Experiment date range
  • Objective/context
  • Misc. notes
